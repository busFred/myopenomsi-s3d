Keys and reading the page
space play / pause · ← → one frame · M layers: lanes / visual edges / both · H height ramp · drag pans, wheel zooms · click pins a vehicle, a person, a lane or a drawn strip The lane layer is the simulation's: every lane's centreline ± width/2. The visual layer is the map's: each spline's .sli cross-section ([profile]/[profilepnt] x and z, textures) laid along the placed spline (position, heading, radius, mirror from the tile), so kerbs are where two profile points share an x at different heights, outer edges are the drawn extent, edges are changes of surface class or height, seams the texture joints within one surface. Crossing objects show their [path] arcs and the plan outline (convex hull) of their first-LOD meshes. Hover a vehicle or a person for its distance to the nearest visual edge (kerb/outer/edge lines, object outlines) and to the nearest lane edge.
